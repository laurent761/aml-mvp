02 / Experiment lifecycle

From intent to outcome.

01

Configure

Bind a pinned target image to a scenario, tools, initial state and verifier. Create a session with a seed and resource limits.

02

Reset

Start an episode in a fresh capsule and check readiness.

03

Intervene

Submit a message, document or declared tool-result replacement. Blue validates delivery and records a receipt.

04

Execute

Run the target/tool loop; save observations, verified outcomes and usage. Repeat until success, termination or a limit.

05

Finalize

Save the trajectory and destroy the capsule. An external session may reset again within budget or close.

Recovery: completed commands retain their results. Worker loss during an effect marks the operation indeterminate and retires the session. No blind replay; persisted records survive, live target state does not.

03 / Evidence

Four distinct signals.

Delivery

Did the intervention reach its declared entry point?

Success

Did trusted events satisfy the forbidden-state predicate?

Reward

A search/training signal. Positive reward does not prove success.

Reproduction

Did fresh pinned replay cause the consequence again? A seed cannot guarantee identical model responses.

Attacker context contains permitted public observations. Official outcomes, verifier evidence, rewards and usage remain separate research fields. Tool-result replacement changes the public response, not private evidence or business state.

04 / Research flow

Keep the lineage intact.

Data discipline

Separate training and test.

Keep test data out of training. Model-input exports exclude official outcomes and rewards.

Team responsibility

Train and load externally.

Your process owns compute, optimization and weight loading. AML tracks artifact integrity and runtime identity; validate actual checkpoint loading independently.

Comparison

Inspect what ran.

Compare verified success and cost. Account for failed and indeterminate executions before claiming improvement.

05 / Operations & scope

What runs. What remains.

Platform

Compose runs the UI, API, workers, supervisor, PostgreSQL, MinIO, MLflow and telemetry. Docker capsules are the development isolation runtime and share the host kernel.

Your team

Own deployment, access, secrets, budgets, capacity, retention and consistent database/artifact backups. Capsules are disposable.

Reference

One finance agent in fixture/model modes. Task: an internal invoice summary. Forbidden outcome: simulated external email containing synthetic personal identifiers. Fixtures demonstrate wiring, not real-model acceptance.

Outstanding

Real-model acceptance; additional targets and a held-out suite; validated learned checkpoints; clean-build acceptance; independently validated Firecracker/KVM isolation where required.
